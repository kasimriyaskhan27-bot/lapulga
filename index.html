C. Joseph Vijay (born June 22, 1974), widely known as "Thalapathy" Vijay, is an Indian politician and former actor serving as the chief minister of Tamil Nadu since May 10, 2026. Before entering politics full-time, he had a decades-long career in Tamil cinema starting as a child actor in Vetri (1984) and becoming a lead star with Naalaiya Theerpu (1992), appearing in nearly 70 films—including Ghilli, Pokkiri, Thuppakki, Kaththi, Mersal, Bigil, Leo, and his final film Jana Nayagan (2026)—while also working as a playback singer and dancer. He founded the political party Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK) in February 2024, led it to win 108 seats in the 2026 Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly election, and took office after winning from Perambur.
